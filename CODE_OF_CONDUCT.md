# Code of Conduct

ModelForge follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct,
version 2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy, credentials and confidential operational data.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets,
and repeated disruptive or bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be made
privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.
